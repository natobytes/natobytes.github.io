Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 May 2026

Who we are

FarmaPerto is an app by NatoBytes, Lda (NIPC 517591367), Argoncilhe, Portugal (“we”, “us”). We are the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. You can reach us at renato.m.alm@gmail.com.

FarmaPerto helps you find on-duty and open pharmacies near you in Portugal. It is an independent app and is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any official body such as ANF, SNS or INFARMED.

In short: FarmaPerto has no accounts and shows no ads. We collect crash diagnostics to keep the app stable. Usage analytics stay off unless you turn them on. Your location is used only on your device to show pharmacies near you — we don’t store it.

What we process and why

DataPurposeLegal basisDefault

We do not require an account and never collect your name, email address or build a profile of you. We show no ads and do not track you across other apps or websites.

Who processes data for us

  • Google — provides Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics. See Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Apple — on iOS, analytics are subject to App Tracking Transparency. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

These providers process data on our behalf as processors, or as independent controllers under their own terms where applicable.

International transfers

Google may process data in countries outside the European Economic Area (EEA), including the United States. Where that happens, the transfer is protected by appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Crash diagnostics: typically up to 90 days (Firebase Crashlytics default).
  • Usage analytics: kept according to our Firebase Analytics settings and only while your consent is active. If you withdraw consent, we stop collecting new analytics.
  • Location: not stored — used momentarily on your device and then discarded.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, the right to data portability, and the right to withdraw consent at any time. You can:

  • turn usage analytics on or off in Settings (Android), or via the tracking prompt and iOS Settings (iOS);
  • manage or revoke location access in your device settings;
  • email us at renato.m.alm@gmail.com for any request.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Portuguese supervisory authority, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD) — www.cnpd.pt.

Children

FarmaPerto is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the “last updated” date above and, for material changes, highlight them in the app or on this page.
